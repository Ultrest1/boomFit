BoomFit Match

Privacy Policy & Support

Effective date: July 13, 2026

Privacy summary: BoomFit Match does not contain advertising and does not track users across apps or websites. Game progress and settings stay on the device. If you use an in-app purchase, purchase history is processed by Apple and RevenueCat to complete, validate, restore, and analyze purchases.

1. Who We Are

BoomFit Match is an iOS and iPadOS puzzle game published by DeerSoft. This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

2. Information Processed

Information stored only on your device

Game progress, coin and life balances, tutorial status, sound and haptic preferences, and similar gameplay settings are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this local gameplay data.

Purchase history

If you view, buy, or restore an in-app purchase or subscription, Apple and our purchase infrastructure provider, RevenueCat, may process:

  • the purchased product identifier;
  • transaction and receipt information;
  • subscription and entitlement status; and
  • a randomly generated, anonymous RevenueCat app user identifier used to associate purchase status with the app installation.

We use RevenueCat's anonymous identifier and do not ask you to create an account. We do not attach purchase history to your name, email address, phone number, advertising identifier, or other real-world identity.

3. How We Use Information

Purchase history is used only for:

  • App functionality: completing and validating purchases, preventing purchase fraud, unlocking premium benefits or coin packages, and restoring purchases;
  • Analytics: understanding purchase performance through RevenueCat's aggregated dashboards and maintaining the purchase experience; and
  • Customer support: investigating a purchase issue when you ask us for help.

4. Sharing and Service Providers

Purchase-related information is processed by:

  • Apple, which operates the App Store and handles payment and transaction processing; and
  • RevenueCat, which validates receipts and manages subscription and entitlement status for the app.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. You can review the Apple Privacy Policy and the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

We do not sell personal information, share it with data brokers, use it for third-party advertising, or use it to track you across apps or websites.

5. Information We Do Not Collect

BoomFit Match does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise or approximate location, photos, camera or microphone data, health data, financial account details, or advertising identifier. The app does not include advertising or social-network tracking SDKs.

6. Retention and Deletion

Purchase records are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to provide purchase and restore functionality, maintain security, resolve support issues, and meet legal obligations. Apple may retain App Store transaction records under its own policies.

You may request deletion of data controlled by DeerSoft or revoke any consent by emailing ultrest12@gmail.com. Because the app does not use named accounts, we may ask for limited transaction or technical details needed to locate an anonymous purchase record. Deletion may be limited where records must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or accounting requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly request or collect personal contact information from children. In-app purchases are handled by Apple and remain subject to the device owner's App Store purchase controls.

8. Security

We use Apple and RevenueCat services to protect purchase information in transit and to validate transactions. No method of electronic processing is completely secure, but we limit processing to what is needed to operate the purchase features.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app or its service providers change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest revision.
